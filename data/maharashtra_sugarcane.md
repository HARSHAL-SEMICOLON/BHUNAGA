# Sugarcane (ऊस) — Maharashtra Advisory (Illustrative)

> SAMPLE document for the FARMER demo. Verify with VSI Pune / MPKV / KVK.

## Planting season
Three main seasons: Adsali (July-August, highest yield, ~18 months),
Pre-seasonal (October-November), and Suru (January-February). Adsali planting
gives the best tonnage where water is assured.

## Spacing and sets
Use 2-3 budded healthy setts. Row spacing commonly 90-120 cm; wider spacing
(150 cm paired rows) suits drip irrigation.

## Water and drip
Sugarcane is water-intensive. Drip irrigation with fertigation saves water and
boosts yield; it is widely promoted in Maharashtra and eligible for subsidy.

## Fertilizer
A general dose is around 250-400 kg N per hectare (variety and season dependent),
with P and K by soil test, applied in splits.

## मराठी सारांश
ऊस लागवडीचे तीन हंगाम: आडसाली (जुलै-ऑगस्ट), पूर्वहंगामी (ऑक्टोबर-नोव्हेंबर) आणि
सुरू (जानेवारी-फेब्रुवारी). ठिबक सिंचनाने पाणी वाचते व उत्पादन वाढते.
