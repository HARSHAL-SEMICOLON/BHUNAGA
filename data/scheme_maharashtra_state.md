# Maharashtra state schemes for farmers

Scheme rules, subsidy rates and deadlines change. Always confirm at your Taluka
Agriculture Office or on the official portal before applying.

## Mahatma Jyotirao Phule Shetkari Karjamukti Yojana

Crop loan waiver for eligible farmers. Applies to short-term crop loans up to a
notified ceiling taken from cooperative and nationalised banks.

Eligibility depends on the loan being outstanding at the cut-off date announced
for the scheme. Verification happens through the bank against Aadhaar.

Farmers who repaid on time have, in some rounds, been given an incentive amount
instead — check whether that component is currently open, as it rewards exactly
the farmers who are usually left out.

## Nanaji Deshmukh Krishi Sanjivani Prakalp (PoCRA)

A World Bank supported project for climate-resilient agriculture in the drought
prone districts of Marathwada and Vidarbha, plus the saline belt of the Purna
river basin.

Support covers farm ponds, drip and sprinkler irrigation, protected cultivation
such as shade nets and polyhouses, sprinkler sets, seed production, and Farmer
Producer Company formation.

Subsidy is typically 65 percent for smallholders and marginal farmers, and 45
percent for others, on approved items.

Applications are made through the DBT portal, and only in villages included in
the project.

## Dr. Panjabrao Deshmukh Vyaj Savlat Yojana

Interest subsidy on short-term crop loans. A farmer who repays a crop loan on
time can effectively receive it at zero or near-zero interest, when combined
with the central interest subvention.

The condition is timely repayment. It is a strong reason not to let a crop loan
run past its due date, since the saved interest is often larger than the penalty
that would be avoided by delaying.

## Magel Tyala Shet Tale (farm pond on demand)

Subsidy for building a farm pond, aimed at protective irrigation during dry
spells. The grant covers a substantial share of digging cost up to a notified
ceiling depending on the pond size.

A farm pond changes what is possible on a rain-fed farm: one protective
irrigation at a critical stage can be the difference between a normal yield and
a failed crop.

## Gopinath Munde Shetkari Apghat Suraksha Sanugrah Anudan Yojana

Accident compensation for farmers and, in the current form, for a family member
working on the farm. Covers death or permanent disability from accidents
including snakebite, drowning, electrocution, and road accidents.

The claim goes through the Taluka Agriculture Officer. Documents needed
generally include the 7/12 extract, FIR or panchnama, post-mortem report where
applicable, and proof of identity and age.

This is one of the least-claimed schemes, largely because families do not know
it exists at the moment they need it.

## Bhausaheb Fundkar Falbag Lagvad Yojana

Support for planting fruit orchards — mango, cashew, guava, pomegranate, custard
apple and others — on the farmer's own land. Assistance covers planting material,
pit digging, fertiliser and plant protection, released across three years so the
orchard is actually maintained rather than just planted.

## Krishi Yantrikikaran (farm mechanisation)

Subsidy on tractors, power tillers, rotavators, seed drills, threshers, harvesters
and plant protection equipment.

Subsidy is generally 50 percent for smallholders, marginal farmers, Scheduled
Caste, Scheduled Tribe and women farmers, and 40 percent for others, subject to
item-wise ceilings.

Applications go through the MahaDBT portal, and selection is by lottery when
applications exceed the target.

## Where to apply

**MahaDBT portal — mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in** — the single window for most
state agriculture schemes. One registration covers many schemes; a farmer can
tick several in one application.

Documents most schemes ask for:

- Aadhaar card
- 7/12 and 8-A land extracts
- Bank passbook, with the account in the applicant's own name
- Caste certificate where relevant
- Recent photograph

**Local help:** the Village Agriculture Assistant (Krishi Sahayak), the Taluka
Agriculture Office, or the nearest Common Service Centre (CSC / आपले सरकार सेवा
केंद्र). Krishi Vigyan Kendras also help with applications and are free.

## A practical warning

No genuine government scheme requires a payment to an agent to apply. Middlemen
charging for MahaDBT applications are common; the portal is free and a CSC
charges only a small notified fee for the service of filling it in.
