# Grapes (द्राक्ष) — Maharashtra Advisory (Illustrative)

> SAMPLE document for the FARMER demo. Verify with ICAR-NRC Grapes, Pune / MPKV.

## Regions and pruning
Nashik, Sangli and Solapur are major grape districts. Grapes follow a double
pruning, single cropping system: foundation (back) pruning around April and
forward (fruit) pruning around October.

## Trellis and spacing
Train vines on a trellis (Y or flat). Spacing varies by variety, commonly around
3 x 1.5 to 3 x 2.4 m.

## Water and disease
Use drip irrigation with fertigation. Downy mildew and powdery mildew are major
diseases, especially in humid/cloudy weather; follow a preventive spray schedule
and ensure canopy airflow.

## मराठी सारांश
नाशिक, सांगली, सोलापूर हे प्रमुख द्राक्ष जिल्हे. एप्रिलमध्ये खरड छाटणी व ऑक्टोबरमध्ये
फळ छाटणी केली जाते. केवडा (downy) व भुरी (powdery) रोगांसाठी प्रतिबंधात्मक फवारणी
करावी.
