## What it is
PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi) is a central scheme giving income
support to land-holding farmer families.

## Benefit
Rs 6000 per year, paid in three equal instalments of Rs 2000, directly into the
beneficiary's bank account (DBT).

## Eligibility (general)
Land-holding farmer families. Certain higher-income groups, institutional land
holders, constitutional post holders, serving/retired government employees above
a pension threshold, and income-tax payers are typically excluded.

## How to apply
Register at pmkisan.gov.in (Farmers Corner -> New Farmer Registration) or at the
nearest Common Service Centre (CSC) / village revenue/agriculture office.

## Documents needed
Aadhaar, bank account details, and land record documents.
