# Soil Health Card (Illustrative)

> SAMPLE document for the FARMER demo. Verify with your local KVK / soil testing
> lab.

## What it is
The Soil Health Card scheme provides farmers a report of their soil's nutrient
status and crop-wise fertilizer recommendations.

## Benefit
Helps apply the right type and quantity of fertilizer, reducing cost and
improving yield. Cards are issued periodically after soil testing.

## How to get it
Submit a soil sample through the local agriculture department / KVK / soil
testing laboratory; the card is generated after analysis.
