# PMFBY — Crop Insurance (Illustrative)

> SAMPLE document for the FARMER demo. Verify on pmfby.gov.in.

## What it is
Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) provides crop insurance against yield
loss from natural calamities, pests, and diseases.

## Farmer premium (general)
Up to 2% of sum insured for kharif food/oilseed crops, up to 1.5% for rabi, and
up to 5% for commercial/horticultural crops. The rest is subsidised by
government.

## How to apply
Through banks (for loanee farmers), CSCs, insurance agents, or the national crop
insurance portal, within the notified cut-off dates for each season.

## Documents
Aadhaar, bank passbook, land records (7/12 / 8A), and sowing declaration.
