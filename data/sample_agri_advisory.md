## Wheat — Fertilizer (Urea)

For irrigated wheat, the general recommendation is about 120 kg Nitrogen per
hectare. Since urea is 46% Nitrogen, that is roughly 260 kg of urea per hectare,
or about 100-110 kg of urea per acre. Apply nitrogen in splits: half at sowing
(basal) and the remaining half in one or two top-dressings at the crown-root
initiation stage (around 21 days) and at the tillering/jointing stage. Always
adjust the dose based on a soil test.

## Cotton — Sowing Time

In most of central and southern India, cotton is sown with the onset of the
monsoon, typically from May to June. Irrigated cotton can be sown a little
earlier. Sowing should be timed so that the crop establishes before heavy rains.
Maintain proper spacing (for example 90 x 60 cm for many hybrids) and treat seed
before sowing.

## Tomato — Late Blight Control

Late blight is a fungal disease (Phytophthora infestans) that spreads in cool,
humid, cloudy weather. Manage it by: using resistant varieties, ensuring good
field drainage and airflow, and removing infected plant debris. For chemical
control, spray a protectant fungicide such as mancozeb, and switch to a systemic
fungicide (for example one containing metalaxyl) when disease pressure is high.
Follow label dosage and pre-harvest intervals.

## Drip Irrigation — Subsidy

Drip (micro) irrigation is supported under government schemes such as
Per Drop More Crop (PMKSY). Farmers can receive a subsidy on the cost of the
drip system; small and marginal farmers generally get a higher subsidy share.
Apply through your state horticulture/agriculture department or online portal,
with land documents and a quotation from an approved supplier. Exact percentages
vary by state and category.

## Paddy (Rice) — Soil pH

Paddy grows well in slightly acidic to neutral soils, with a suitable soil pH
range of about 5.5 to 6.5. It tolerates a wider range, but strongly acidic or
alkaline soils reduce yield. For acidic soils below 5.5, liming can help; for
alkaline/sodic soils, gypsum and proper drainage are used.
