# Drip / Micro-Irrigation Subsidy — PMKSY (Illustrative)

> SAMPLE document for the FARMER demo. Verify with your state horticulture/
> agriculture department.

## What it is
Under Per Drop More Crop (PMKSY-PDMC), farmers get a subsidy on drip and
sprinkler (micro-irrigation) systems to save water and improve yield.

## Subsidy (general)
Small and marginal farmers generally receive a higher subsidy share than other
farmers. Exact percentages vary by state and category.

## How to apply
Apply through the state agriculture/horticulture department or the state's
micro-irrigation portal, with a quotation from an approved/empanelled supplier.

## Documents
Land records, Aadhaar, bank details, and a supplier quotation.
