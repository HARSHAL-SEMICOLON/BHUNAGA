# Onion (कांदा) — Maharashtra Advisory (Illustrative)

> SAMPLE document for the FARMER demo. Verify with ICAR-DOGR Rajgurunagar / MPKV.

## Seasons
Three crops in Maharashtra: Kharif (monsoon), Late Kharif, and Rabi. Rabi onion
(transplanted around December-January) stores best and gives high yield.

## Nursery and transplanting
Raise nursery and transplant seedlings at 6-8 weeks. Spacing about 15 x 10 cm.

## Fertilizer
General dose around 100-110 kg N, 40-50 kg P2O5, 50-60 kg K2O per hectare, with
sulphur which improves bulb quality and storage.

## Storage
Cure bulbs properly after harvest and store in well-ventilated structures to
reduce losses. Avoid excess late nitrogen, which hurts storage life.

## मराठी सारांश
महाराष्ट्रात खरीप, उशिरा खरीप व रब्बी असे तीन हंगाम. रब्बी कांदा साठवणुकीस उत्तम.
गंधक (sulphur) दिल्यास कांद्याची प्रत व साठवण सुधारते.
