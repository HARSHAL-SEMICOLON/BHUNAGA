# Soybean (सोयाबीन) — Maharashtra Advisory (Illustrative)

> SAMPLE document for the FARMER demo. Verify with ICAR-IISR / MPKV / KVK.

## Sowing
Soybean is a kharif crop sown in June-July after 75-100 mm of rainfall, when the
soil has enough moisture for germination. Avoid sowing on a single light shower.

## Spacing and seed rate
Row spacing 45 cm, plant-to-plant 5 cm. Seed rate around 65-75 kg/ha. Treat seed
with Rhizobium and PSB culture plus a fungicide.

## Fertilizer
Being a legume, soybean needs less nitrogen. A general basal dose is about
30 kg N, 60-75 kg P2O5 and 30 kg K2O per hectare.

## Disease
Yellow mosaic virus (spread by whitefly) and root rot are common. Use tolerant
varieties and manage whitefly.

## मराठी सारांश
सोयाबीन जून-जुलैमध्ये ७५-१०० मिमी पाऊस झाल्यावर पेरावी. ओळींतील अंतर ४५ सेमी.
बीजप्रक्रिया रायझोबियम व बुरशीनाशकाने करावी.
