# Cotton (कापूस) — Maharashtra Advisory (Illustrative)

## Sowing time
In Maharashtra, rainfed (kharif) cotton is sown with the onset of the monsoon,
usually from June to early July. Irrigated cotton (especially in Vidarbha and
Khandesh) may be sown a little earlier. Aim to sow before heavy continuous rain.

## Spacing and seed
For Bt hybrids, common spacing is 90 x 60 cm (rainfed) or wider under irrigation.
Use certified seed and treat seed with recommended fungicide/insecticide.

## Fertilizer
A general dose for hybrid cotton is around 100-120 kg N, 50 kg P2O5 and 50 kg K2O
per hectare. Apply nitrogen in 2-3 splits. Adjust by soil test.

## Pest management
Watch for pink bollworm and sucking pests (aphids, jassids, whitefly). Use
pheromone traps for pink bollworm monitoring, follow refuge planting, and avoid
extending the crop too late into the season. Spray only at economic threshold.

## मराठी सारांश
कापसाची पेरणी जून ते जुलैच्या सुरुवातीला मान्सूनच्या आगमनानंतर करावी. संकरित
वाणांसाठी ९० x ६० सेमी अंतर ठेवावे. नत्र खत २-३ हप्त्यांत द्यावे. गुलाबी बोंडअळी
व रस शोषक किडींवर लक्ष ठेवावे.
